Previous Work on Use of 2D Vision for Obstacle Detection on Mobile Phones

A Computer Vision tutorial exploring how mobile devices can help blind and low-vision users detect obstacles in their surroundings.

About This Tutorial

Individuals who are blind or have low vision may face challenges detecting obstacles while navigating unfamiliar or crowded environments. Mobile phones equipped with cameras can provide a practical platform for computer vision systems that analyze the surrounding environment and identify potential obstacles.

This tutorial reviews previous work involving two-dimensional computer vision for mobile obstacle detection. It also examines object detection techniques such as YOLO, mobile system design, existing research, limitations, and future improvements.
